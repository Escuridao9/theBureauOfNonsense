      Clerk's handbook / Edition 04

      A stamp is a responsibility.

      1. Read the current department's regulations. Rules change between shifts.
      2. Compare every regulated field with the rulebook. An applicant's statement is flavour, not evidence.
      3. Approve applications that meet every rule. Reject the others and select every broken rule.
      4. A correct decision earns 10 points. A fully justified inspection earns another 5.
      5. Normal has no timer. Under Pressure gives you 20 seconds per application, including rejection reasons; a missed deadline earns no points. Main menu pauses the clock.
      6. Never-ending Paperwork has no timer. The third wrong verdict ends your run. Incomplete rejection reasons only cost bonus points.
      7. Your progress and the Roll of Honour stay in this browser.
